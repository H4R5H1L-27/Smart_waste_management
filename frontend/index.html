Municipal Waste Stream Directory

Official Chennai Corporation Segregation Guidelines

BLUE BIN Plastic Waste

Clean bottles, food containers, packaging films. Rinse and remove screw caps.

GREEN BIN Paper & Cardboard

Dry office paper, newspapers, flattened cardboard boxes. Must be kept clean and dry.

CYAN BIN Glass Recyclables

Beverage bottles, cosmetic jars, broken glass. Rinse thoroughly before disposal.

YELLOW BIN Metal & Cans

Aluminum beverage cans, tin food cans, foil trays, cookware. Compact when possible.

BROWN BIN Organic / Wet Waste

Vegetable peels, fruit waste, food scraps. Diverted to municipal composting plants.

RED BIN Sanitary / Non-Recyclable

Non-recyclable packaging, composites, soiled materials routed to sanitary landfill.
